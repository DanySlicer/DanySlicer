Rooted in craft.

Founded by designer Aiko Nakamura, Hikari Kicks collaborates with artisans across Japan — from dye masters in Kyoto to leather smiths in Kobe. Each pair is hand-numbered, celebrating the makers who bring them to life.

Our atelier honors the balance of wabi-sabi, embracing natural textures and subtle asymmetry to create sneakers that feel lived-in from the first step.

Craft Notes

Every silhouette follows the rhythm of Japanese seasonal festivals.

春 — Haru Bloom

Organic cotton dyed with sakura petals and sealed with rice-bran wax for weather resistance.

夏 — Natsu Ember

Breathable mesh infused with binchotan charcoal keeps each step cool during humid nights.

秋 — Aki Harvest

Hand-stitched leather overlays echo the warm hues of Momiji foliage in Kyoto.

冬 — Fuyu Dawn

Waterproofed suede finished with pearl dust to shimmer like snow on Mount Fuji.
